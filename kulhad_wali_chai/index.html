Our Story and Our Legacy

Since 1980, Kulhad-wali-chai has been brewing authentic Indian tea, preserving a heritage passed down through generations. Our recipes rely on hand-selected, real Indian spices sourced directly from different regions across the country, bringing together the rich, aromatic flavors of India's diverse spice culture in every sip. We proudly serve our tea in handcrafted clay kulhads—a tradition rooted deep in Indian culture that honors age-old brewing methods while remaining 100% eco-friendly and sustainable.
